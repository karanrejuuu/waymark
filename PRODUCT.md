# PRODUCT.md — Waymark

## Truth
- **Product:** Waymark
- **Category:** FMCG Wholesale Replenishment & Delivery Route Operations PWA
- **Audience:** Indian FMCG distributors, wholesale agency owners, tempo delivery drivers, and kirana supply salesmen.
- **Operating Context:** Field delivery rounds across dense Indian markets (galis, wholesale mandis, bazaar lanes), often in basements with zero mobile reception, bright midday sunlight, one-handed operation on budget Android devices.
- **Primary Problem:** Indian FMCG distributors lose 15-20% of replenishment orders because shop restock intervals rely on paper diaries and memory. When a shelf empties for even 48 hours, competing brands take the rack facing.
- **Solution:** Offline-first, self-adjusting daily dispatch line with instant cash ledger and dues collection at the tempo door.
- **Platform:** Web (PWA / Mobile responsive Next.js).
- **Stack:** Next.js 14 (App Router), Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, Dexie (IndexedDB), Supabase (Auth, Postgres, RLS).
